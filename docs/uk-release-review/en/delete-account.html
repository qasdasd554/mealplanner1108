Before publication

Confirm the actual scope of deletion, retained transaction records, backup retention and support response times. The published Polish page promises immediate application deletion, up to 30 days for backups and different seven-day email deadlines; these are source claims, not verified infrastructure guarantees. The source page's live email-generation form is described here but is deliberately not executable in this review draft.

Delete your account in the application (recommended)

The source page states that, in line with Apple App Store and Google Play Data Safety requirements, you can immediately delete your account and its associated data directly in Meal Planner Polska:

  1. Open Meal Planner Polska on your iOS or Android phone.
  2. Go to Profile, the tab at the bottom right.
  3. Scroll down and select Delete account.
  4. Confirm the operation. Your account and associated data will be permanently deleted.

Request deletion by email without opening the application

If you have uninstalled the application or no longer have access to your phone, you can request account and data deletion by writing to kontakt@mealplannerpolska.pl.

The Polish source also provides a local email-message generator. It asks for the account email address and optional additional information, then opens your email application with a prepared request. You must send that message yourself; opening the email application does not submit a deletion request. No active form or message generator is included in this review draft.

Suggested message:

Hello,
Please permanently delete my Meal Planner Polska account and its associated personal data.
Account email: [your account email]
Additional information: [optional]
Kind regards

What is deleted, and when?

  • Scope: the source page lists the user's email address, dietary profile, created meal plans, own recipes (including AI-scanned recipes), shopping lists, and calorie and hydration diary history.
  • Timescale: the source page states that deletion in the application is immediate and backups are cleared within a maximum of 30 days. It states that email requests are completed within seven working days. The source generator's confirmation separately says up to seven days; this difference requires review.
  • Active subscriptions: deleting an application account does not automatically cancel a subscription managed by Apple or Google. To stop renewal, use Apple account settings → Subscriptions or Google Play → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions.
