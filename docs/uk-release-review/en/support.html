Support centre — Meal Planner Polska

Have a question about the application, want to report a bug or suggest a feature, or need help with your account? Contact kontakt@mealplannerpolska.pl.

The source page says replies are usually sent within 24 hours on working days. For account and privacy matters, see the account deletion instructions. In the application, go to Profile → Delete account.

Frequently asked questions

1. How do I create a meal plan for a selected shop?

From the application's home screen, open the new meal plan builder, select your preferred shop, set the duration (1–14 days), diet and number of people. The application creates the meal plan and shopping list. The Polish source gives Biedronka, Lidl, Carrefour and Dino as examples; those examples are not a claim about UK retailer coverage.

2. How does AI recipe scanning work?

In Recipes, choose the option to add a recipe with AI. Take a photo of a written recipe, paste recipe text or provide a cooking website link. The AI identifies ingredients and quantities and calculates calories and nutritional values.

3. How do I manage or cancel Premium?

Subscription payments are handled by the store where you obtained the application:

  • iPhone / iPad: open Settings → your name / Apple Account → Subscriptions → Meal Planner Polska.
  • Android: open Google Play → profile icon → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Meal Planner Polska.

4. How do I report inappropriate content or a product error?

Use the reporting action in the application or email kontakt@mealplannerpolska.pl. The source page states that moderation reports are checked within 24 hours.
