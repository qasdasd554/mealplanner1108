2. Application services

  • Meal planner and diet builder: meal plans tailored to dietary preferences (no restrictions, vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, keto or high-protein), duration (1–14 days) and number of people.
  • Recipe and nutrition catalogue: recipes with calculations for energy (kcal), protein, fat and carbohydrate.
  • Price comparison and shopping lists: indicative product prices at popular retailers in Poland (including Biedronka, Lidl, Carrefour and Dino) and interactive shopping lists.
  • Calorie, nutrient and hydration diary: daily calorie balance, physical activity and water intake.
  • AI recipe assistant: conversion of handwritten recipe photos, text or links into structured recipes and shopping lists.
