1. Data controller

This Privacy Policy sets out the rules for processing and protecting the personal data of users of the Meal Planner Polska mobile application (available from the Apple App Store and Google Play) and the website mealplannerpolska.pl, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (GDPR).

For matters concerning the protection of personal data, contact kontakt@mealplannerpolska.pl.

2. What data do we process, and why?

  • User account data (email address and account identifier): processed to create and manage an account, synchronise meal plans and shopping lists between devices, and handle support requests (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of a contract).
  • Dietary preferences and food diary: selected diets (such as vegetarian, keto or gluten-free), excluded allergens, calorie targets, recorded meals and hydration levels — processed solely to personalise meal plans and operate the application's calorie calculator.
  • Recipe photos and text submitted to the AI scanner: when you use Add a recipe with AI (a photo of a recipe, text or a link), the submitted material is processed solely to identify ingredients and preparation steps and calculate nutritional values. The application accesses the camera or photo library only at your express request.
  • Technical and diagnostic data: anonymous information about application stability and technical errors, used to improve the software (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — the controller's legitimate interests).

3. Mobile device permissions (iOS / Android)

  • Camera and photo library: used only when you choose to scan a written recipe or add a meal photo. You can withdraw access in your phone's system settings at any time.
  • Push notifications: optional meal and hydration reminders, enabled only with your consent.

4. Local storage and the application simulator

The simulator's state is held in the current browser tab and resets when the page is refreshed. localStorage records your decision about optional website analytics for 180 days. If your browser blocks this storage, the decision applies only until the page is closed or refreshed.

4a. Optional website analytics

Once the administrator has configured the service and you have consented, the website uses Google Analytics 4. Measurement covers visits, page views and clicks on App Store and Google Play links, including the position of the button on the page. Clicking a link does not confirm that the application was installed.

The Google Analytics code is not downloaded before consent. After consent, Google may store analytics cookies such as _ga and _ga_* and process technical measurement data. Advertising personalisation and Google Signals are disabled in the website configuration. Website events do not include form contents or URL query parameters.

You can decline measurement or withdraw consent through Analytics settings in the website footer. Withdrawal stops further measurement in that browser and removes Google Analytics cookies accessible to the website; it does not delete data already sent. See Google's privacy policy for information about the provider.

5. Data recipients and security

Data transferred between the application and the server is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS. We use trusted providers of cloud infrastructure, authentication systems, secure transactional email delivery (Resend), and AI text/image processing for recipe extraction, who guarantee compliance with GDPR requirements. We never sell users' personal data to third parties.

6. User rights (GDPR) and account deletion

Each user has the right to:

  • access their data and obtain a copy;
  • rectify inaccurate data;
  • permanently delete their data (the right to be forgotten), directly in the application (Profile → Delete account) or by following the account and data deletion instructions;
  • restrict processing and exercise data portability rights;
  • complain to the President of the Polish Personal Data Protection Office (PUODO).

English privacy summary from the source page

Data collection and purpose: Meal Planner Polska collects only the data needed for meal planning, shopping list synchronisation, calorie tracking and AI recipe scanning: an email address for authentication, user-selected dietary preferences, and user-submitted recipe photos or text for AI processing.

No third-party tracking or data selling: we do not sell personal data to third parties or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

Retention and instant deletion: you can permanently delete your account and all associated personal data at any time in the application (Profile → Delete account) or through the account deletion page.
